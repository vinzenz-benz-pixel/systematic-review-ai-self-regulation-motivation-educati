# Inclusion Criteria

## Research Focus: Self-Regulated Learning and Learning Processes
- Studies that focus on self-regulation in the learning process or on dimensions of self-regulated learning.
- Studies that examine learning processes, motivation, learning strategies, or self-management in educational or training contexts.
- Studies that analyze the influence of AI on self-regulation, learning behavior, or the design of learning processes.

## Educational Context
- Studies conducted in educational or training environments, including K–12 education, higher education, vocational education and training (VET), workplace learning, and professional development.
- Studies taking place in school, academic, or professional learning contexts.

## Artificial Intelligence
- Studies that examine the use of AI technologies, including generative AI, large language models (LLMs), machine learning, predictive analytics, recommender systems, and intelligent tutoring systems.
- Studies with a clearly identifiable AI component in the educational process.

## Learning Analytics and Data Use
- Studies involving the collection, analysis, monitoring, visualization, interpretation, or prediction of learner data, including learning analytics, educational data mining, dashboards, and predictive models.
- Studies in which learning or behavioral data are used to understand or support learning processes.

## Educational Function
- Studies in which AI-supported analyses are used to inform, support, or improve educational practices, for example by monitoring learning progress, identifying learning patterns, or providing feedback.
- Studies with clear educational relevance for teachers, learners, or institutional decision-making processes.

## Publication Type
- Peer-reviewed journal articles, conference papers, empirical studies, mixed-methods studies, qualitative studies, quantitative studies, design-based research, case studies, and quasi-experimental studies.
- Studies with systematic empirical evaluation.

## Language
- Publications in English or German.

## Publication Period (optional)
- Studies published between 2015 and the review search date, reflecting the rapid development of AI in education.

## Target Users
- Studies involving learners, teachers, trainers, mentors, school or organizational leaders, educational institutions, or multiple educational stakeholders as target groups.

---

# Exclusion Criteria

## Research Focus: Self-Regulated Learning and Learning Processes
- Studies that do not focus on self-regulation, learning processes, or learning strategies.
- Studies that focus exclusively on technical or non-educational aspects of learning without a connection to self-regulation or the design of learning processes.

## Educational Context
- Studies conducted exclusively in non-educational areas, such as healthcare, business, finance, marketing, manufacturing, or other sectors without a learning component.
- Studies without an educational or training context.

## Artificial Intelligence
- Studies that focus exclusively on traditional digital technologies or learning analytics without an identifiable AI component.
- Studies that do not use AI as a core component of the intervention or analytical approach.

## Learning Analytics and Data Use
- Studies that use AI only for content generation, translation, material creation, or as a tutor, but do not analyze learner data or learning processes.
- Studies without the use of data to analyze learning behavior, learning progress, or learning processes.

## Educational Function
- Studies focused exclusively on technical model development, algorithm benchmarking, system architecture, or software development without a clear educational application.
- Studies without a direct connection to educational practices, decision support, or learning support.

## Publication Type
- Editorials, commentaries, opinion articles, news articles, book reviews, abstracts without a full study, and non-scholarly publications.
- Publications without empirical or systematic evaluation.

## Language
- Publications in languages other than English or German, unless a complete English or German full-text version is available.

## Publication Period (optional)
- Studies published before 2015.

## Target Users
- Studies without educational stakeholders or in which AI is not intended to support educational stakeholders.
- Studies that do not address learners, teachers, or other relevant educational stakeholders.
