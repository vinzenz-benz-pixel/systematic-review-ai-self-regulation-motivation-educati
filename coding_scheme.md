# Coding scheme
## Hybrid Intelligence for Supporting Motivational Self‑Regulation in Education

This coding scheme is designed to support a theory‑driven systematic review focusing on
(a) motivational self‑regulation as the target construct and  
(b) hybrid intelligence as the functional distribution of regulatory roles between humans and AI systems.
